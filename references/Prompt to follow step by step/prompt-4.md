4. Visual Design

Follow the visual direction of the supplied references:

Premium Indian textile showroom aesthetic.

Warm ivory, beige, muted gold, and deep forest-green accents.

Elegant typography with excellent readability at a distance.

Large saree previews and clear product imagery.

Generous spacing, refined borders, subtle decorative details, and clean component layouts.

Large touch targets suitable for customers standing in front of a kiosk.

Consistent branding and visual language across all three steps.

The finished experience should resemble a dedicated digital saree trial room, not a conventional website squeezed into a kiosk.

Do not invent a completely new visual design when the reference images already define the intended design.
