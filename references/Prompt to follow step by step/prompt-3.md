3. Lenovo Yoga Laptop Testing

Development and testing will initially be performed on a Lenovo Yoga laptop in portrait orientation, using its touchscreen and flip functionality.

The laptop is a development and testing device, not the primary design target.

The interface must therefore work correctly in portrait orientation on the laptop while remaining optimized for the much larger physical kiosk screens.

Support portrait viewport dimensions that differ from the final kiosk.

Keep the entire workflow usable at laptop portrait resolutions.

Ensure buttons, text, cards, and images remain appropriately sized.

Handle browser viewport changes and orientation changes without breaking the layout.

Avoid making the interface unnecessarily tiny just to fit everything onto the laptop.

If the viewport becomes too short, adapt the layout sensibly instead of overlapping or cutting off important controls.

Do not prioritize mobile phone layouts or desktop landscape layouts. The primary target is a portrait touchscreen kiosk, with Lenovo Yoga portrait mode as the development and testing environment.
