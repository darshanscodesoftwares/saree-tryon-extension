6. Expected Workflow

The customer journey must remain simple:

Step 1 — Choose Saree
The customer browses the collection, views individual sarees prominently, and selects one or multiple designs. Clearly indicate which sarees have been selected.

Step 2 — Your Photo
The customer positions themselves in the camera preview and initiates capture. Display a prominent five-second countdown before capturing the photo, following the existing POC logic.

Step 3 — Virtual Try-On
Display the customer's virtual try-on result prominently. When multiple sarees have been selected, customers can switch between their results using the selected-saree thumbnails or appropriate swipe gestures. Do not display the unwanted directional controls described above.

Allow customers to return to the collection when needed and continue through the existing workflow without losing their relevant selections or photo data.

The layout must also account for the case where only one saree is selected.
